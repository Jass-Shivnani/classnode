Account & Data Deletion

Delete Your ClassNode Account and Data

In accordance with Google Play and Apple User Data policies, you can request the permanent deletion of your account and all associated personal data.

⚠️ Warning: Deleting your account is permanent. All your cloud-synced classes, attendance history, assignments, and shared group memberships will be permanently deleted from our servers within 30 days.

How to Delete in the App (Instant)

You can instantly delete your account directly from your device:

  • Open the ClassNode app.
  • Tap your Profile Avatar / Settings in the top header.
  • Scroll down and tap "Delete Account".
  • Confirm deletion. Your authentication record and cloud data will be wiped immediately.
