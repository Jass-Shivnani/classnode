ClassNode Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

1. Overview

ClassNode ("we", "our", or "us") is dedicated to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile and desktop applications collect, use, store, and safeguard your personal information when you use ClassNode.

2. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: When signing in with Google or Email, we collect your name, email address, and profile photo provided by your authentication provider.
  • Academic Data: Class schedules, subject names, attendance records, assignments, and notes that you enter or sync.
  • Cloud & Drive Data: If you enable Google Drive sync, note attachments and backups are stored securely within your own Google Drive application folder.
  • Crash & Performance Logs: Anonymous crash reports and performance metrics collected via Firebase Crashlytics to diagnose bugs and improve app stability.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide core class tracking, attendance calculations, and timetable scheduling.
  • To sync your class information across your own devices and with authorized classmates in shared study groups.
  • To prevent crashes and optimize application performance.
  • We do NOT sell your personal data to third parties.

4. Third-Party Services

ClassNode relies on trusted industry-standard infrastructure:

  • Google Firebase: Authentication, Cloud Firestore synchronization, and Crashlytics. (Firebase Privacy Policy)
  • Google Drive API: User-controlled note backup and storage. (Google Privacy Policy)

5. Data Retention & Deletion

Your data is stored locally on your device and in your cloud storage for as long as you maintain your account. You can permanently delete your account and all associated data at any time from within the app settings or by visiting our Account & Data Deletion Request Page.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at support@classnode.app.
